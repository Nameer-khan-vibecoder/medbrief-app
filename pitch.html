The Problem

Every visit starts from zero.

Patients carry folders of paper files between hospitals. When they switch doctors, records get lost, tests get repeated, and drug interactions go undetected — because context never travels with the patient.

📂
Scattered paper records
Prescriptions, lab reports, X-rays — all in a physical folder. Easy to lose. Impossible to share instantly.
🔁
Duplicate tests & prescriptions
Doctor 2 doesn't know what Doctor 1 prescribed. Patients repeat tests — wasting time and money.
🚨
Undetected medication risks
Medication errors are a major preventable patient-safety issue in Indian hospitals; prescription and administration errors are the most common.
